The Opportunity

A Greenhouse
for Community

Acadiana has no community anchor — no gathering place designed for everyone, open all day, every day. The Greenhouse on Moss fills that void. And during the hours young people need belonging most, it becomes something more: a protected place for teens to connect, play, and grow.

The Problem

Community is Fragmenting — and Teens Feel It Most

Lafayette has no third place — no shared gathering space between home and work. Loneliness is rising across all ages. But teens bear the sharpest edge: the WHO identifies ages 13–17 as the loneliest group globally (20.9%). In Louisiana, 26% of juvenile crime occurs between 2–6 PM. Youth recreation costs have surged 46% since 2019, pricing out families who need community most.

Our Solution

A Multi-Use Community Clubhouse with a Purpose

A climate-controlled greenhouse event hall, restored pool, gaming lounge, botanical gardens, and café — open to the entire community, all day, all week. Mornings for seniors, remote workers, and families. Evenings for community events, workshops, and rentals. Weekends for parties, markets, and performances. And every weekday from 3–6 PM: dedicated, protected hours for teens.

Our Amenities

Four Distinct Destinations
Under One Roof

Spanning a nearly 3-acre historic estate, The Greenhouse on Moss offers a curated collection of spaces — each designed to serve the full community across every hour of the day, every day of the week.

The Grand Hall

A landmark climate-controlled greenhouse event space featuring a café bar, botanical gardens, movie nights, live acoustic music, and private event hosting. The heart of the estate — open to everyone.

The Game House

Billiards, ping pong, foosball, console and PC gaming lounge, board game library, and a quiet homework nook. Open to all members — and during dedicated after-school hours, a protected space where teens can decompress, connect, and just be.

Pool & Outdoor Recreation

Restored pool, sun deck, loungers, splash pad, and supervised swim sessions. Friday night pool parties and outdoor summer programming make this the go-to summer destination.

Events & Programming

Birthday parties, HOA community nights, speed dating events, family movie screenings, networking gatherings, wellness workshops, and 8-week summer camp sessions. A full, living calendar that serves every generation — year-round.
